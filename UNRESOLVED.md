# UNRESOLVED

Escalations from the build. One entry per item. An agent writes here instead of
editing `ledger.json`, weakening a unit to fit, or starting a fourth round.

Format:

## [unit id] — [gate or pass that surfaced it] — [date]
**What blocked it.** One paragraph.
**What I tried.** Rounds 1–3, briefly.
**The three legal fixes and why each fails.** Rewrite the text / insert a rung /
move the unit later. If one of them works, this entry shouldn't exist.
**What I think this is telling us.** Usually one of: the unit wants to be two
units; a rung is missing in front of it; the term budget is wrong; the sequence
order is wrong here.
