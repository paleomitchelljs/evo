threshold A neuron fires when the number of its excitatory inputs that are firing, minus the number of its inhibitory inputs that are firing, reaches this number. Set to 0, it fires with no input at all, until something holds it down. 1
Sensors on the left fire while their stimulus is there. Click one to switch it on or off. Muscles on the right contract while the one neuron wired into them fires. Drag from the dot on the right edge of a sensor or neuron onto a neuron or a muscle to connect them. A new connection excites (arrow). Click it once to make it inhibit (bar); click again to remove it. Click a neuron to set its threshold or remove it; drag it to move it. Every cell is either firing (filled) or silent (open), and signals run one way, out of the dot and into the next cell. A wire that would loop back is refused.

Every combination One row for each way the stimuli can be on (●) or off (○). For each muscle, the column on the left is what the animal should do; the one on the right is what your wiring does. Click a row to set the sensors to it.
