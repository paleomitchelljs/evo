S23 — The cloud and the histogram are the same cloud

Lesson 4 built a cloud of slopes by resampling data. Lesson 10 built a histogram of allele frequencies by random copying. They look like different pictures from different units. They are the same object: a sampling distribution, and both tighten at the same rate, 1/√n. Set the sample size once and watch the two collapse together.
