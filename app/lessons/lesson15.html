Locked — confirm your name above to begin.
  • A season on the island: garter snakes hunt salamanders, and hawks hunt snakes. You are the snake outlined in red; steer with the mouse.
  • The hawks come early in the season and take every snake above ten: a hawk's shadow falls where its snake is heading, so get out of it. Then 30 salamanders come out over the season, and a young snake costs 2. A snake that eats nothing dies before winter. At winter you choose a mate; each pair has as many young as their salamanders pay for.
  • Every snake carries three chromosomes from each parent, with three genes on each. A coloured dot is an allele: dots on a snake's left flank come from its mother, on its right flank from its father. Plain green is the allele each gene started with.
  • About one new allele turns up somewhere in each generation. None of them does anything.
  • Next season you play one of your young.
  • Pedigree, right: every snake that has lived, one row a generation, its two sets of chromosomes. Point at a snake to see its parents and young; point at one gene to follow that copy back through the generations. Yours are outlined.
