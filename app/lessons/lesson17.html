        Set 1Four populations of N genomes: orange, blue, red and purple. At the start each is all its own colour, one allele a gene. Nothing helps or harms.Set 1: the four populations, each with a bar of its mix of colours; under them, the four pooled.
        
        
      
        
      
        
        
        Set 1: how differentiatedSet 1 on the diagram's square: each population's colours gene by gene (a column a gene) and its heterozygosity; on each line, FST between those two; in the middle, the four pooled and FST.Heterozygosity: the share of a genome's genes at which its two chromosomes are different colours, averaged over the population's genomes.FST: how different the populations are from each other. 0: every population the same mix at every gene. 1: each only its own colour.
