Locked — confirm your name above to begin.
  • Two islands of finches, long apart, now with birds crossing between them. Three genes: one for beak size, one for colour, one for the colour a female likes. See whether a difference in what the females like helps the two islands' beaks stay apart.
  • Click the seeds in the Target card to set how many of each island's seeds are hard. Small beaks crack soft seeds, large beaks hard ones.
  • Preference strength, the arrow from preference on the diagram, sets how much a female favours a male of the colour she likes.

Colour → mating follows it: dashed at 0, where his colour makes no difference to mating. Grey arrows: fixed.
