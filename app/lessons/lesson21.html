The clade's change, taken apartEvery 50 generations the change in the clade's average splits into three parts that add up to it exactly (Price's equation, one level up: species in place of fish). Added up since generation 0, and averaged over the 16 clades.Between species: species with more dispersers leave more species, or fewer (the covariance of a species' share with the number of species it leaves).Inside species: the change in each species' own share as its fish live and breed.At the splits: how a new species' share differs from its parent's when it begins.

Each generation it stays cut off, dispersers from either side cross it with a chance that grows with how many there are and how far they go: the piece rejoins. A piece cut off for 40 generations is a new species (Lesson 19 C).Each species is lost with chance 0.002 a generation, whatever it carries; at most 40 species.
