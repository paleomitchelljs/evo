        Survival, births, fitnessMale sticklebacks show a throat colour when they breed: red in limnetic fish, often black in benthic fish (Boughman 2001).Here every fish prefers a partner whose throat looks like its own: it turns a partner down with a chance that grows with how different their throats are, up to 4 times, then takes the next it meets. A fish turned down has fewer births.throat gene → throat colour: where one red and one black allele look. Fitness is not set anywhere: it comes out of survival and births.
