Locked — confirm your name above to begin.
  • One population of grey genomes splits in two: orange on the left, blue on the right, 25 hermaphrodite genomes each. Each genome: two chromosomes of 40 genes, drawn as beads, as in Lesson 17.
  • While apart, each population takes new mutations in its own colour: orange on the left, blue on the right. Nothing helps or harms at home.
  • Before the run, some pairs of genes were made incompatible, 2 in every 100: an orange mutation at one gene with a blue mutation at the other, in the same genome, halves its fitness. Each incompatible pair it carries halves it again.
  • Then the two come back into contact: each generation, each genome swaps places with one in the other population with chance m, for 100 generations.
  • A hybrid: a young of one orange and one blue genome. Its grid: across, the genes where it carries a blue mutation; down, an orange one; red, a pair that is incompatible.
  • FST: how different the two populations are, as in Lesson 17. 0: the same mix at every gene. 1: nothing in common.
  • Draw on the over-time plot: drag across it to set a dot every 25 generations, your guess at what the menu's measure will do.
  • Go plays the split and the contact in 50 pairs of populations; pair 1 is drawn up close. The shaded cloud: the middle 90% of the 50 at each generation. A dot inside it turns green, outside it red.
  • Practice, ticked at the start: your own settings, draw or not, nothing counts. Untick it for round 1 of 4: each round holds its settings and what to draw.
  • Your runs: every run adds two dots at its time apart. Your predictions: each drawing's dots inside the cloud.
