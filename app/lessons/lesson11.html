A heterozygote is an individual with two different genetic ancestors for that locus.

  • Four hundred individuals, one locus, two alleles. Each individual is two halves, one per copy.
  • Nothing dies and nothing is selected. The only control is who pairs with whom.
  • F is the gap between the two-tone individuals you can count and the two-tone individuals random pairing would have given.

R code

# 400 individuals, two copies each, at one locusp <- 0.5          # how common the dark allele isf <- 0            # how often a partner is a relativeHo <- mean(a != b)   # two-tone individuals, countedp  <- mean(c(a, b))   # the frequency they implyHe <- 2 * p * (1 - p)  # what random pairing would giveF  <- 1 - Ho / He
