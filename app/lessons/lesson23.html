Locked — confirm your name above to begin.
  • A clade of species, 200 birds each. Now and then a barrier cuts a small piece, 10 birds, off a species. A piece that stays apart 40 generations is a new species; one that meets its species, and whose birds mate across, rejoins it. See how a choosiness allele fares inside each species, and across the clade.
  • A choosy female mates less often with a male whose colour is unlike the one she likes, as in lesson 21; a piece's colour drifts away from its species' while it is apart. Cost of choosiness, the red arrow: a choosy bird leaves fewer young. Preference strength, the arrow onto the colour gap: how much a choosy female avoids a male of the other colour.
