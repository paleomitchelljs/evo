Decoded

Score counts the 1-bits across all three question groups. Active is time on task with idle gaps discarded; elapsed is the raw wall clock, so a tab left open over lunch inflates the second and not the first. Moves is how many times the student actually worked the controls, per stage — an engagement figure, not a score. A row flagged edited failed its checksum: the code was altered after it was issued, or it was typed out by hand with a slip in it.
