Grant finch data — citation
===========================

The files in this directory are derived from the data package accompanying:

    Grant, P. R. & Grant, B. R. (2014).
    40 Years of Evolution: Darwin's Finches on Daphne Major Island.
    Princeton University Press.

Island: Daphne Major, Galápagos
Period: 1973–2012 (annual observations)

Derived files
-------------

finch_beak.csv / finch_beak.json
    Annual mean beak measurements (mm) for Geospiza fortis and G. scandens.
    Columns: year, species, beak_length, beak_depth, beak_width.
    Source figures: Fig. 01-06 (fortis), Fig. 01-07 (scandens).

finch_pop.csv / finch_pop.json
    Annual population counts on Daphne Major for the two species.
    Columns: year, fortis, scandens.
    Source figure: Fig. 14-03.

Notes
-----

The raw CSVs that these were derived from ship with the book as companion
data and are not redistributed in this repository. If you use these data
for anything beyond the course exercises, please cite the Grants' book.
